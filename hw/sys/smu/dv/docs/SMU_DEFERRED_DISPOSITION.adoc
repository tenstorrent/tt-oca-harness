[[smu-deferred-disposition]]
== SMU deferred and OUT disposition

This table is the v0.5.0 source of truth for every name that was catalogued
in the removed `testlists/deferred.toml` ledger; with that file gone, this
document is the record. None of these names is in `sep0_all`. Nine of them
are also enrolled in wrapper `all` on the SEP=1 track
(`smu_sep_smoke_test` plus eight `sep_real_fw` images); a raise stub must
not be reported as PASS. The remaining bodies live under
`cocotb/tests_deferred/`, each carrying its blocker in its docstring.

Buckets:

* `restore-for-v0.5.0` — implement and enroll before OSS signoff. None today.
* `OUT-until-SEP1` — needs real SEP under SMU. Owner is the SEP=1 track (#493).
* `child-TB-owned` — protocol or CPU depth belongs to the SMC, SEP, or DTP TB.
* `waived` — superseded, toggle-as-pass, stimulus-pin gap, or leftover catalog name.

Related enrolled-set work that is *not* a restore from this catalog:

* The enrolled no-Force JTAG2AXI / OTP / filter set is `sep0_all` and is recorded in `SMU_SEP0_COMPONENT_SIGNOFF.adoc` (#490). ATOP reject is OUT (#491).
* `smu_ext_boot_seq_gate_test` and `smu_smc_mailbox_int_test` are enrolled in `sep0_all` (`testlists/wrapper.toml`). They are not in this deferred catalog.

Live group counts in `testlists/wrapper.toml`, `README.md`, and `SMU_VPLAN.adoc`
must stay aligned: `all` = 81, `sep0_all` = 53, deferred catalog = 123.

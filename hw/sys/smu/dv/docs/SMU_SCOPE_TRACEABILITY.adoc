Executable membership on `main` today, `--dut smu` only:

`sep0_all` and the SEP=1 groups are two evidence surfaces on one DUT:
`--dut smu` is the wrapper (`testlists/wrapper.toml`), and CI runs its
`sep0_all` nightly and weekly. PASSes carry `DUT_TAG=WRAPPER`. The residual
block bench is `--dut smu_block` (`testlists/all.toml`, two leaves), whose
PASSes carry `DUT_TAG=BARE` and are not feature credit. See #489.

[[smu-credit-rules]]
=== Credit rules

* Feature credit requires a named `TEST_EVIDENCE` token the sequence
  already emits and a non-vacuous `SmuScoreboard` count. The map must not
  invent a checkbox the body cannot prove.
* Credit is the enrolled `sep0_all` set. `smu_ext_boot_seq_gate_test` and
  `smu_smc_mailbox_int_test` stay PR-only until #493 / #481 decide
  otherwise.
* Every name in `SMU_DEFERRED_DISPOSITION.adoc` is OUT. Raise stubs are
  not reportable as PASS.
* Wrapper PASSes (`DUT_TAG=WRAPPER`) are OUT of this matrix.
* SEP=1 instantiate / fetch / retire (F2) and live SMC↔SEP mailbox
  challenge-response (F5) are OUT (#493).
* The commercial names `main_wrapper` and `main_wrapper_por` are not in
  this tree. Wrapper successors are
  `smu_wrapper_elaboration_no_sep_test` and
  `smu_wrapper_elaboration_sep_rtl_test`. See #261.

[[smu-dv-verification-plan]]
== SMU OCAH Open-Source Verification Plan

=== Overview

[cols=",",options="header",]
|===
|Field |Value
|Design Under Test |`hw/top/smu_wrapper.sv` through `hw/sys/smu/dv/tb/tb_wrapper_top.sv`
|Framework |PyUVM on cocotb
|Primary simulator |Verilator
|Reference testbench architecture |`SMU_TB_ARCH.adoc`
|Executable contract |the enrolled groups in `testlists/all.toml` (`--dut smu`)
|===

This plan describes what the SMU open-source suite verifies and against which
RTL. The executable contract is the enrolled testlist groups; this document
describes them, it does not replace them.

=== Verification Strategy

The SMU is verified at its own boundary on the production wrapper
(`hw/top/smu_wrapper.sv`, one compile profile, `compile_smu_chiplet`): the
SMC, DTP and SEP below it are real RTL, the SEP runs its EL2 core, and the
external SMN fabric is driven by the shared AXI VIP. Four surfaces are covered
on the wrapper, plus the SEP firmware set:

. *Fabric and address decode* — external SMN AXI into SMC, ID-width conversion,
  crossbar error handling, ATOP rejection, alias remap and the inbound and
  outbound filters, driven both from the external port and through JTAG2AXI.
. *SMC under SMU* — bring-up, reset control, mailbox, watchdog, GPIO straps and
  the security demote path, reached through the SMU rather than at the SMC
  boundary.
. *DTP under SMU* — primary JTAG, STAP selection, boundary-scan and iJTAG, the
  OTP bridge and the cross-trigger routing, as they appear once DTP sits under
  the SMU.
. *Clock, reset and boot sequencing* — clock-stop coordination, boot stall,
  IC_RESET domains and external boot-sequence gating.
. *SEP firmware under SMU* — SEP boot and firmware execution, lifecycle state
  broadcast from the SEP eFuse shadow to the SMC and the DTP, and the entropy
  stack, with the verdict taken from the firmware's own terminal loop.

Checking is centralised in `SmuScoreboard`, which refuses a vacuous PASS and
requires every mapped evidence token to have been proven by a real check. See
the checking strategy in `SMU_TB_ARCH.adoc`.

=== Naming Rules

[cols=",",options="header",]
|===
|Rule |Requirement
|Single-scenario module |Use the module name, for example `smu_clock_stop_coordination_test`
|Multi-scenario module |Use `+smu_<feature>_<scenario>_test+`, for example
`smu_axi_filter_in_instance_matrix_test`
|Scenario names |Use descriptive lower-snake-case names that encode operation,
mode, direction or coverage target
|Names from another subsystem |Normalise to `+smu_*+` and keep the target inside
the name, for example `smu_smc_wdt_sanity_test` for the SMC watchdog reached
through the SMU
|Open-source files |One PyUVM test class per scenario under
`cocotb_wrapper/tests/`, so each scenario is independently selectable from
a testlist. Sequences live in `cocotb/seq_lib/` (those written against the
SMU's own interfaces) and `cocotb_wrapper/seq_lib/` (those written against
the wrapper's), two halves of one namespace package, where scenarios differ
only in stimulus
|===

=== BFM Policy

The BFM table lives in `SMU_TB_ARCH.adoc` so the testbench chapter and the plan
cannot drift apart. The policy itself: standard protocols use the shared OCAH
VIP packages, SMU-specific behaviour uses OCAH-local models under `env/`, and
no enrolled test may reach into the DUT with Force or deposit.

=== Verification Scope

This plan claims SMU behaviour at the `smu_wrapper` boundary, with the DUT
elaborated on the `compile_smu_chiplet` profile (`SEP=1`, the real SEP core,
the open-source IP integration attached). The behaviours below sit inside a block another
plan owns, behind a pad the bench does not model, or on a path the tree has
no stimulus for; a green SMU regression says nothing about them, and
`hw/sys/smu/doc/dv/SMU_DEFERRED_DISPOSITION.adoc` records the disposition of
every name that was catalogued against them.

[cols="1,2",options="header",]
|===
|Behaviour |Why it is out of scope here
|The SMC's internal behaviour (CSRs, peripherals, firmware boot beyond the smoke images) |The SMC plan, `hw/sys/smc/dv/docs/SMC_VPLAN.adoc`; this plan proves what crosses the SMU boundary and the SMC-under-SMU paths
|The SEP's internal behaviour, its crypto blocks and its lifecycle-controller policy |The SEP plan, `hw/sys/sep/dv/docs/SEP_VPLAN.adoc`; this plan observes the SEP through the wrapper's boot, lifecycle and JTAG-gating outputs
|The DTP's block-level behaviour (TAP controller, JTAG2AXI bridge internals, iJTAG instruments) |The DTP plan, `hw/sys/dtp/dv/docs/DTP_VPLAN.adoc`; this plan proves the DTP-under-SMU paths the wrapper exposes
|Boundary-scan cells, adopter DFD and DFT scan chains |No cells in the wrapper; the bench loops every host scan chain back on itself (an approved stand-in)
|The eFuse macros |`efuse_bank_model` answers every sense behind the shim (an approved stand-in)
|The asynchronous cold-reset assertion path |The RTL de-glitches the assertion; the scenario is catalogued in the disposition record with the RTL ruling it waits on
|Live SMC↔SEP mailbox challenge-response and SEP=1 fetch/retire as feature credit |Firmware-driven SEP scenarios need the toolchain container; they run in `all`, not in the scheduled `hosted` set, and count as observe-only
|The SEP=0 composition, `smu #(.SEP(0))`: the direct ID-width converters in place of `smu_axi_xbar`, the `lc_state` and demote tie-offs, the SEP=0 OTP error slave |Not verified in this tree: no target of this package elaborates SEP=0. The names that prove that shape are catalogued `not-runnable` in `hw/sys/smu/doc/dv/SMU_DEFERRED_DISPOSITION.adoc`, bodies in git history, and that record owns them until a plan builds the elaboration they need
|===

[[smu-credit-rules]]
==== Credit rules

* Feature credit requires a named `TEST_EVIDENCE` token the sequence
  already emits and a non-vacuous `SmuScoreboard` count. The map must not
  invent a checkbox the body cannot prove.
* Credit is the enrolled `hosted` set.
* Every name in `hw/sys/smu/doc/dv/SMU_DEFERRED_DISPOSITION.adoc` is OUT and reports no PASS.
* The SEP=0 composition is OUT of this matrix: no target elaborates it, and
  its names are `not-runnable` in the disposition record.
* SEP=1 instantiate / fetch / retire (F2) and live SMC↔SEP mailbox
  challenge-response (F5) are OUT.
* Wrapper elaboration / reset is `smu_wrapper_elaboration_test`
  (wrapper `fw_boot` and `elaboration`).

*Objective*: The inbound SMN port carries several transfers in flight and refuses the burst types the register targets do not support.

*Test Coverage*:

* With the SMC local aperture routed and an INBOUND0 window opened over JTAG2AXI, sixty-four reads of `VERSION_LO` under distinct ARIDs are launched while RREADY is held low. * Sixty-four writes under one AWID are launched against `SCRATCH_COLD` while BREADY is held low; the AW handshakes accepted before the first B handshake are counted the same way and at least two are required. * A WRAP burst read and a multi-beat FIXED burst write at a register target are refused with SLVERR; the passing response is the error, and the preceding OKAY probes on the same window are the live control that shows the path is open.

. Arm an AW/B accept counter, hold BREADY low, issue sixty-four writes to `SCRATCH_COLD` under one AWID; require at least two AW handshakes before the first B handshake, then read the register back and compare against the last value written.

*Source Module*: `smu_periph_ext_window_test` (`cocotb_wrapper/tests/`)

*BFM / Model*: `ocah_jtag_vip` (SMC fabric JTAG2AXI); `ocah_axi_vip` for the out-of-chiplet leg; `elaboration` firmware mode

*Objective*: The SMC adopter external window is reachable over JTAG2AXI at the addresses the SMC memory map and the register map generated from `smc.rdl` give, answers at the two blocks those sources place, returns DECERR on the first page above everything they allocate, and shrinks with the SMC aperture.

*Test Coverage*:

* EFUSE_SHIM_CTRL.EFUSE_BANK_INIT_TIME, at the EFUSE_SHIM_CTRL block base the generated SMC map gives, is read at its RDL reset value over the eFuse bank-control AXI-Lite port, written, read back and restored, so the shim window is proven both ways.
* The straps pair in the supplementary region answers, and the first 4 KiB page above every allocation the sources record, still inside the window, returns DECERR.
* REGION_SIZE shrunk so the local aperture ends exactly at the window sends the same shim read out of the chiplet instead, observed on the outbound boundary rather than inferred from the response alone. * Golden sources: the window base and size from `hw/sys/smc/regs/gen/c/smc_addr.h` (`SMC_TOP_SMC_EXTERNAL_*`) and the mandatory / supplementary split from `hw/sys/smc/doc/memmap.adoc` ("AXI-Lite External Window"); the EFUSE_SHIM_CTRL block base, both region bases and the allocated extent from the register map generated from `smc.rdl` for the boot ROM (`hw/sys/smc/bootrom/prod/registers/smc_top_regs.h`); the EFUSE_BANK_INIT_TIME offset and reset from the PeakRDL headers of `hw/ip/efuse/dv/models/regs/efuse_shim_ctrl.rdl`; the straps addresses transcribed from `hw/sys/smc/bootrom/prod/doc/hardware-initialization.adoc` ("Straps and eFuses"), with `straps.rdl` fixing STRAPS_HI at STRAPS_LO + 4; the REGION_SIZE and LOCAL_BASE resets from `smc_base_config.h`. The sequence refuses to run if these sources disagree about the window.
* Specification gap: `hw/sys/smc/doc/memmap.adoc` leaves every placement inside the window to the adopter and `hw/ip/efuse/doc/memmap.adoc` says the SHIM sits at no fixed address, so the shim base rests on the generated boot-ROM map (which describes a vendor shim layout; only the block base is taken from it, and the register inside follows `efuse_shim_ctrl.rdl`) and the straps address on the boot-ROM documentation. Neither is an SMC memory-map statement. The map passes the unallocated remainder through to the adopter external port without saying what answers it; `hw/sys/smu/doc/port_table.adoc` ties an unused port to DECERR, and the DV rule is that an address no source allocates must not be answered by a live register. `CHK-PERIPH-EXT-UNMAPPED` is graded against that rule, not against a decode boundary a document states.

. Shrink REGION_SIZE so LOCAL_BASE + size ends at the window and repeat the shim read; require the transaction on the outbound boundary.

*BFM / Model*: `ocah_jtag_vip` on the primary TAP; TB loops each host scan_out/TDO back to its scan_in/TDI at the wrapper pins; `elaboration` firmware mode

* Expected behaviour comes from the JTAG specification pages, not the RTL. *Test Coverage*:

* Register offsets, bitmasks and the CONFIG.MODE reset value come from `hw/ip/cross_trigger/cross_trigger_port/regs/gen/c/` and `hw/ip/cross_trigger/cross_trigger_matrix/regs/gen/c/`; the CTP count, the CTM port count and every per-index CTP and CT_SRC address come from `hw/ip/cross_trigger/cross_trigger_network/regs/gen/c/cross_trigger_network_addr.h`. * The lock stage is what makes the demote stages non-vacuous: a register that simply stored whatever software wrote would satisfy every demote check. Only a *refused* write after lock shows the block implements the policy.
* *Not claimed*: a demote is not required to move `feat_ctrl`. [[sec-105-smu_smc_efuse_shadow_sense_reset_test]]

*Source Module*: `smu_smc_efuse_shadow_sense_reset_test` (`cocotb_wrapper/tests/`)

*BFM / Model*: `efuse_bank_model` loaded from `dv/assets/smc_efuse_all_ones.hex`; TB pin drive of `rst_cold_ni`; `elaboration` firmware mode; run mode `sep_rtl_fuse_sense`, which leaves `+skip_fuse_sense` unset

*Objective*: After a real SMC fuse sense, `smc_shadow_regs_o` carries the sensed eFuse image at every register offset `smc_addr.h` gives the map, and it reads the zero every `smc_efuse_map.rdl` field resets to while `rst_cold_ni` is held; the image programs every fuse bit, so both samples are taken on the full width of the port.

*Test Coverage*:

* The sense is the claim, so the leaf runs on the fuse-sense run mode and requires `+skip_fuse_sense` to be absent; `smc_fuse_sense_done_o` has to be observed rising 0 -> 1, and a run that begins with it already high fails.

*Source Module*: `smu_axi_in_attribute_sweep_test` (`cocotb_wrapper/tests/`)

*BFM / Model*: `ocah_axi_vip` master on the flat `ext_in_*` pins and the shared responder on `u_axi_out_if`; `ocah_jtag_vip` for the inbound filter entries, the SMC aperture and the outbound write; `elaboration` firmware mode

*Objective*: Every AW/AR attribute the inbound SMN port carries is answered as the traffic-filter specification assigns, and one SMC write leaves the chiplet on `smu_axi_out`.

*Test Coverage*:

* `hw/ip/axi_filter/doc/index.adoc` "Match, Then Permit" lists the whole match condition: `entry_enabled`, the address range after granule widening, `prot[1] == allow_ns`, the source ID, and `allow_burst = 1 or AxLEN = 0`. === Signoff Package

The SMU signoff package is this book plus the tracker: the feature list,
the scope and traceability matrix, the release matrix, the component scope
and the disposition record (`hw/sys/smu/doc/dv/SMU_DEFERRED_DISPOSITION.adoc`)
hold across candidates, and the tracker's signoff item carries what belongs to
one candidate (the commit, the run trees it was qualified with, the open
issues and their release dispositions). Feature credit is the
<<smu-scope-traceability,Scope and Traceability>> matrix above; the feature
list it grades against follows.

[[smu-feature-list]]
==== SMU hosted feature list

This chapter defines the hosted feature subset F1–F7 and maps every enrolled
test to one feature and one `TEST_EVIDENCE` token. The F1–F7 numbering is
defined by the table below and nowhere else; there is no separate SMU feature
specification in this tree. The hardware facts the features describe are in
`doc/integrator/src/smu.adoc`.

The executable contract is `testlists/all.toml`. Feature credit is
the enrolled `hosted` group. Names in `hw/sys/smu/doc/dv/SMU_DEFERRED_DISPOSITION.adoc`
and SEP=1 work are OUT.

|===

|Feature-credit group |`hosted` (`--dut smu`)

|Evidence map |`cocotb/env/smu_evidence_map.py` `TEST_EVIDENCE`

|===

[[smu-known-limitations]]
=== Known Limitations

* *`all` is not a scheduled group.* `.github/workflows/regress.yml` runs
  `hosted` nightly and weekly: the rest of `all` is the SEP firmware set,
  whose `c_build` stages need the RISC-V toolchain container, which the
  hosted runners do not have. Those leaves run only where a developer or a
  runner with the container runs `--items all`; the sign-off run of record
  is such a run, not a workflow run.
* *The wrapper is the surface.* `--dut smu` is the wrapper bench and carries
  `hosted` itself; its PASSes are labelled `DUT_TAG=WRAPPER` and are the
  density evidence. The SEP=0 composition (`smu #(.SEP(0))`) has no target in
  this package, so nothing here verifies it; its proofs are `not-runnable` in
  the disposition record.
* *Deferred scenarios are not cards.* Names classified deferred or OUT --
  including the SEP-driven SMC bring-up images that poll SRAM no sys-inbound
  master can reach, the JTAG2AXI abort scenario, the SEP=0 composition
  proofs, and the
  asynchronous cold-reset assertion the RTL does not implement -- are
  catalogued in `hw/sys/smu/doc/dv/SMU_DEFERRED_DISPOSITION.adoc` with the
  condition each needs, and have no entry in this plan or in any testlist.
* *Cross-trigger and iJTAG have no OCAH-local model*, so the coverage those
  interfaces get is what the DTP-under-SMU tests reach through JTAG and the
  bench's pad loopbacks.
* *No functional coverage closure target is set.* Coverage intent, the
  metric choice and what this plan may claim from a database are in
  `SMU_FCOV.adoc`; the wrapper's policy file is
  `cov/config/verilator/smu_wrapper_coverage_policy.toml`.
* *Verilator replaces the `och_prim` `prim_sync2` / `prim_sync3`
  cells with the behavioural stand-ins in
  `hw/sys/smc/dv/tb/verilator_stubs/`.* VCS drops them. Xcelium receives
  both files; which of the pair it elaborates is not established. The
  leaves that observe a crossing are named in <<smu-bench-standins>>.

[[smu-bench-standins]]
==== Bench stand-ins

The `prim_sync2` / `prim_sync3` files listed in `smu_sim_cfg.toml`
`[build].stubs` are product-module stand-ins, not tooling shims. They
elaborate ahead of the Bender filelist so Verilator's `-Wno-MODDUP`
first-definition-wins selects them; when `tool == "vcs"` the
runner drops any stub whose basename the Bender graph supplies, and the
product cells elaborate. Xcelium receives both files like Verilator does;
no Xcelium build of this bench is recorded, so which of the pair Xcelium
elaborates is not established by this record. The behaviour the stand-in
adds (`initial '0` on
each stage), the leaves that observe a crossing
(`obs_powergood_stable_o`, `obs_powergood_stable_low_seen_o`, the
reset-release observes of `smu_smc_reset_ctrl_test` and
`smu_ext_boot_seq_gate_test`, and the boot-stall gate of the five
boot-stall leaves), and the condition that closes the entry are the
`prim_sync2` / `prim_sync3` stand-ins row of
`hw/sys/smu/doc/dv/SMU_DEFERRED_DISPOSITION.adoc` (*Bench stand-ins and
exceptions*). No VCS control run with the product cells is recorded on
this composition.

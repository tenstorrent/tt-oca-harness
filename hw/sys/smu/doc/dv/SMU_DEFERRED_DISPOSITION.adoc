[[smu-deferred-disposition]]
== SMU deferred and OUT disposition

This table is the catalog of every SMU test name outside the SEP=0 credit
set `sep0_all`. Two kinds of name are in it. Eleven have a test body and are
enrolled in `testlists/wrapper.toml`; their bucket says which group holds
them and why that group is not credit. The rest have no test body in this
tree and are not reportable as PASS.

Buckets:

* `SEP1-enrolled` — has a body and runs in the wrapper `all` group on the `sep_rtl` profile with real SEP instruction retirement (`sep_real_fw`, or `smoke` for the SEP smoke). Not `sep0_all` credit, and no CI workflow schedules those groups.
* `SEP1-blocked` — has a body, enrolled in `sep_smc_sram_blocked` outside `all`: the SEP-driven SMC bring-up polls SMC SRAM that no sys-inbound master can reach, so the leaf cannot pass by construction.
* `OUT-until-SEP1` — no body; needs real SEP instruction retirement under SMU and SEP=1 TB ports.
* `child-TB-owned` — protocol or CPU depth belongs to the SMC, SEP, or DTP TB.
* `waived` — superseded, toggle-as-pass, stimulus-pin gap, or leftover catalog name.

Enrolled names outside this catalog:

* The JTAG2AXI / OTP / filter set in `sep0_all` is recorded in `SMU_SEP0_COMPONENT_SIGNOFF.adoc`, which also records ATOP reject as OUT.
* `smu_ext_boot_seq_gate_test` and `smu_smc_mailbox_int_test` are enrolled in `sep0_all` (`testlists/wrapper.toml`).

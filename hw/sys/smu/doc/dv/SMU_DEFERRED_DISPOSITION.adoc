[[smu-deferred-disposition]]
== SMU deferred and OUT disposition

This chapter has two parts. The table below is the catalog of every SMU test
name classified deferred or OUT, and none of those names is in the hosted
credit set `hosted`; <<smu-bench-standins>> is the record of every
stand-in, force, skip and waiver on a proof path of an enrolled test. The
table first. Three kinds of name are in it. Fifteen have a test body and are
enrolled in `testlists/all.toml`; their bucket says which group holds them
and why that group is not credit. Seven have a test body under
`hw/sys/smu/dv/cocotb_wrapper/tests/` or `cocotb/tests/` and are enrolled in
no testlist, because they cannot run or cannot pass on either bench; their
bucket says what each needs. The rest have no test body in this tree. None of
the last two kinds is reportable as PASS. Enrolled names outside `hosted`
that carry neither classification — the lifecycle, debug-gating, chain and
elaboration leaves — are not in this table: each is carded in
`SMU_VPLAN.adoc` and enrolled in a named group of `testlists/all.toml`.

Buckets:

* `SEP1-enrolled` — has a body and runs in the wrapper `all` group with real SEP instruction retirement (`sep_real_fw`, `sep_entropy`, or `fw_smoke` for the SEP smoke). Not `hosted` credit, and no CI workflow schedules those groups.
* `SEP1-blocked` — has a body under `cocotb_wrapper/tests/`, enrolled in no testlist: the SEP-driven SMC bring-up polls SMC SRAM that no sys-inbound master can reach, so the leaf cannot pass by construction. Re-enrols in `all` the day the SMC SRAM path exists.
* `not-runnable` — has a body, enrolled in no testlist: the claim it checks is not met by the RTL as built, or the bench cannot produce the condition it needs. The row names the condition.
* `OUT-until-SEP1` — no body; needs real SEP instruction retirement under SMU and SEP=1 TB ports.
* `child-TB-owned` — protocol or CPU depth belongs to the SMC, SEP, or DTP TB.
* `waived` — superseded, toggle-as-pass, stimulus-pin gap, or leftover catalog name.

Enrolled names outside this catalog:

* The JTAG2AXI / OTP / filter set in `hosted` is recorded in `SMU_HOSTED_COMPONENT_SIGNOFF.adoc`, which also records ATOP reject as OUT.
* `smu_ext_boot_seq_gate_test` and `smu_smc_mailbox_int_test` are enrolled in `hosted` (`testlists/all.toml`).
* The one unenrolled body under `cocotb/tests/`, `smu_ext_axi_global_addr_smoke_test`, is in the table below under `not-runnable`.

|OTP-vs-fabric concurrency on the MAP shadow word
|waived
|`smu_otp_vs_fabric_map_race_test` carries a `_race_` name it does not earn, and this row is the reason it keeps it. Both writers are JTAG2AXI bridges behind the one TAP, so their DR shifts are serialised by construction and the OTP op has completed before the fabric write can be issued; the enrolled test therefore claims back-to-back coherence, not overlap, and says so in its header and plan entry. Genuine overlap needs a second, independent master on the shadow-map word, which the wrapper's product interfaces do not provide. Not renamed here because the name is carried by the testlist, the evidence map, the feature list and the plan anchor, and the rename would ride inside a branch already this large; it belongs in a standalone change.

[[smu-bench-standins]]
=== Bench stand-ins and exceptions

A stand-in is anything that answers in place of the mechanism a claim is
about: a testbench force, a simulation-only path selected by a plusarg, a
configuration waiver, a loopback where a chain would be, a compiled-in
substitute for a product cell, or a model inside the product composition.
Seven sit on proof paths of enrolled SMU tests. Each has one entry of six
fields. The first three -- the claim that is accepted with the stand-in in
place, why the real path cannot be used, and the exact scope -- are filled
from the tree. The last three -- approver, approval date against a revision,
and review or expiry date -- are filled by a person; until they are, the entry
is a declaration and clears nothing, and every test on its scope is graded as
if the stand-in were undeclared. `pending` in those fields means exactly that.
Each entry describes the tree at this branch's head, not an older revision:
the stand-ins and the entries that declare them move in the same commits, so a
hash written into this file is stale the moment the file is edited. What makes
an entry checkable instead is the command in <<smu-standin-recheck>> -- one per
entry, each naming a string a reader can see for themselves. An approver fills
the `Date, revision` field with the revision they checked and the command they
ran.

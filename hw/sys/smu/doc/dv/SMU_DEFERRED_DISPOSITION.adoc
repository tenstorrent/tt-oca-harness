[[smu-deferred-disposition]]
== SMU deferred and OUT disposition

This chapter has two parts. The table below is the catalog of every SMU test
name classified deferred or OUT, and none of those names is in the hosted
credit set `hosted`; <<smu-bench-standins>> is the record of every
stand-in, force, skip and waiver on a proof path of an enrolled test. The
table first. Two kinds of name are in it. Thirteen have a test body and are
enrolled in `testlists/wrapper.toml`; their bucket says which group holds
them and why that group is not credit. The rest have no test body in this
tree and are not reportable as PASS. Enrolled names outside `hosted` that
carry neither classification — the lifecycle, debug-gating, chain and
elaboration leaves — are not in this table: each is carded in
`SMU_VPLAN.adoc` and enrolled in a named group of `testlists/wrapper.toml`.

Buckets:

* `SEP1-enrolled` — has a body and runs in the wrapper `all` group on the `sep_rtl` profile with real SEP instruction retirement (`sep_real_fw`, or `smoke` for the SEP smoke). Not `hosted` credit, and no CI workflow schedules those groups.
* `SEP1-blocked` — has a body, enrolled in `sep_smc_sram_blocked` outside `all`: the SEP-driven SMC bring-up polls SMC SRAM that no sys-inbound master can reach, so the leaf cannot pass by construction.
* `OUT-until-SEP1` — no body; needs real SEP instruction retirement under SMU and SEP=1 TB ports.
* `child-TB-owned` — protocol or CPU depth belongs to the SMC, SEP, or DTP TB.
* `waived` — superseded, toggle-as-pass, stimulus-pin gap, or leftover catalog name.

Enrolled names outside this catalog:

* The JTAG2AXI / OTP / filter set in `hosted` is recorded in `SMU_HOSTED_COMPONENT_SIGNOFF.adoc`, which also records ATOP reject as OUT.
* `smu_ext_boot_seq_gate_test` and `smu_smc_mailbox_int_test` are enrolled in `hosted` (`testlists/wrapper.toml`).

[[smu-bench-standins]]
=== Bench stand-ins and exceptions

A stand-in is anything that answers in place of the mechanism a claim is
about: a testbench force, a simulation-only path selected by a plusarg, a
configuration waiver, a loopback where a chain would be, a compiled-in
substitute for a product cell, or a model inside the product composition.
Six sit on proof paths of enrolled SMU tests. Each has one entry of six
fields. The first three -- the claim that is accepted with the stand-in in
place, why the real path cannot be used, and the exact scope -- are filled
from the tree. The last three -- approver, approval date against a revision,
and review or expiry date -- are filled by a person; until they are, the entry
is a declaration and clears nothing, and every test on its scope is graded as
if the stand-in were undeclared. `pending` in those fields means exactly that.
Each entry describes the tree at this branch's head, not an older revision:
the stand-ins and the entries that declare them move in the same commits, so a
hash written into this file is stale the moment the file is edited. What makes
an entry checkable instead is the command in <<smu-standin-recheck>> -- one per
entry, each naming a string a reader can see for themselves. An approver fills
the `Date, revision` field with the revision they checked and the command they
ran.

==== `+skip_fuse_sense` with the shadow preload

[cols="1,4"]
|===
|Claim accepted
|On the leaves that pass it, the SMC eFuse shadow registers -- and on the `sep_rtl` profile the SEP ones -- hold the image the preload file supplies, and the sense FSM that would have earned that image from the eFuse macro is skipped. The consumers of the sensed image are under test on those leaves: the lifecycle controller, `lc_state` at the SMC and `dbg_disable` at the DTP, the OTP word the JTAG2AXI leaves read, and the reset and boot state downstream of the fuse-reset release. The sense FSM is not. The five boot-stall leaves (`smu_boot_stall_jtag_cold_reset_matrix_test`, `smu_boot_stall_vs_ic_reset_priority_test`, `smu_clock_stop_coordination_test` S1/S2, `smu_dft_dtp_boot_stall_test`, `smu_dft_gpio_boot_stall_test`) are not on this scope: they run without the plusarg, so the `smc_fuse_reset_n_delayed_o` release they gate is the SMC eFuse controller's own sense completion against the `+smc_efuse_hex` image, and their gate claim is judged on that run. `smu_smc_reset_ctrl_test`, `smu_ext_boot_seq_gate_test` and `smu_smc_smoke_test` read the reset and boot state downstream of the release the boot-stall leaves prove; the OTP leaves (`smu_dtp_otp_smc_map_rw_test`, `smu_dtp_otp_smc_complete_rw_test`, `smu_dtp_otp_smc_series_error_test`, `smu_otp_vs_fabric_map_race_test`) compare against a shadow word that was preloaded. The lifecycle leaves (`sep_lifecycle`, `sep_chain`) declare their `+sep_shadow_reg_preload` image as the stimulus, so for them the preload is setup, not a stand-in. `smu_sep_lcc_flow_test` is not on this scope either: it runs without the plusarg, the SEP shadow registers it observes are the SEP eFuse controller's own sense of the `+sep_efuse_hex` image, and its sense claim is judged on that run.
|Why the real path cannot be used
|On the block bench `tb/tb_top.sv` ties the eFuse shim command and bank-controller responses to zero, so no sense can complete and primary reset would never release. On the wrapper the real path is available -- `hw/top/smc_ip_integration.sv` instantiates `efuse_interface_shim` and `efuse_bank_model`, and the boot-stall leaves use it -- so for the remaining wrapper leaves the skip is a run-time and stimulus convenience: the SMC sense of the 768-word image takes 2280-2330 `clk_smu` after primary reset release on Verilator, and the preloaded shadow image, not a sensed one, is the value the OTP and lifecycle leaves declare as their input.
|Scope
|`testlists/wrapper.toml`: 82 of 88 entries carry `+skip_fuse_sense` and `+smc_shadow_reg_preload=hw/sys/smc/dv/assets/default_efuse_shadow_reg.preload` in their own `args`, each above a one-line reason; the `sep_rtl` entries other than `smu_sep_lcc_flow_test` add `+sep_shadow_reg_preload=assets/default_sep_efuse_shadow_reg.preload`, and the lifecycle leaves name their own image. The `[run_modes.*]` tables of `smu_sim_cfg.toml` carry no eFuse plusarg. `smu_block_sim_cfg.toml` `[run_modes.smoke]`, `[smc]`, `[dtp]`, `[fabric]` still pass both, for the block-bench reason above. The plusarg is live only when the `SIMULATION` define is set (`hw/common/dv/configs/profiles/native.toml` sets it on every target); a build without it senses. `tb/tb_wrapper_top.sv` exports the SEP shadow registers' `sim_skip_fuse_sense` flag as `sep_fuse_sense_skipped_o`, and the SMC shadow registers print `Skipping fuse sense` or `Not skipping fuse sense` at time zero, so a log tells which path ran.

|Why the real path cannot be used

==== `prim_sync2` / `prim_sync3` stand-ins

[cols="1,4"]
|===
|Claim accepted
|Every synchroniser output the bench observes on Verilator is produced by a two- or three-stage flop pipe that starts at a defined `0` with no reset, not by the `och_prim` cells the product elaborates; the product cells are on no Verilator proof path. The claims downstream -- power-good stability, primary and cold reset release, the boot-stall gate, `i2c_enable` into the LSIO mux -- are accepted as proven over the stand-in's behaviour.
|Why the real path cannot be used
|The product `prim_sync2` / `prim_sync3` start with X on the first stage and the wrapper port exposes no `rst_ni` to clear it, so on a four-state view the X persists into every consumer; the stand-ins add `initial '0` on each stage and change nothing else about the pipe.
|Scope
|`smu_sim_cfg.toml` and `smu_block_sim_cfg.toml` `[build].stubs` list `hw/sys/smc/dv/tb/verilator_stubs/prim_sync2.sv` and `prim_sync3.sv`; the runner emits them ahead of the Bender filelist so Verilator's first-definition-wins picks them, and drops them on VCS. Every leaf of both benches compiles them in. Downstream observes on this bench: `obs_powergood_stable_o`, `obs_powergood_stable_low_seen_o`, the reset-release observes of `smu_smc_reset_ctrl_test` and `smu_ext_boot_seq_gate_test`, and the boot-stall gate of the five boot-stall leaves. ==== eFuse models in the product composition

[cols="1,4"]
|===
|Claim accepted
|The OTP word the JTAG2AXI OTP leaves read and write, and the eFuse image the SEP lifecycle controller consumes, are answered by `efuse_bank_model` behind `efuse_interface_shim` inside the wrapper's IP integration, not by eFuse macros. Command decode, series and single-op sequencing, error responses and the shadow-register path through the shim are DUT logic and are proven; the macro's storage, sense timing and programming are not.
|Why the real path cannot be used
|The eFuse macro is a technology cell outside the open-source tree; the composition instantiates the model in its place for every simulator.
|Scope
|`hw/top/smc_ip_integration.sv` instantiates `u_efuse_interface_shim` (`efuse_interface_shim`) and `u_efuse_bank_model` (`efuse_bank_model`); `hw/top/sep_ip_integration.sv` instantiates the same pair for the SEP.

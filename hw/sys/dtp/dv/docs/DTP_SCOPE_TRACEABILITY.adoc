[[dtp-scope-traceability]]
== DTP scope and traceability

This chapter is the requirement-to-test-to-coverage matrix for the debug
and test ports (DTP). The requirements are the designer-owned design
documentation under `hw/sys/dtp/doc/` and the JTAG and cross-trigger IP
chapters it includes; `DTP_VPLAN.adoc` states what each canonical scenario
verifies; `DTP_FCOV.adoc` states the coverage intent; live group membership
is `testlists/all.toml` and the per-area lists it includes. This file is the
reconciliation between them: which scenarios count as feature credit, which
names are compatibility aliases that count for nothing, and which
requirements have no credit on this bench.

[cols=",",options="header",]
|===
|Field |Value
|DUT |`--dut dtp` → `dtp` under `dtp_uvm_top` (`tb/tb_top.sv`); cocotb (PyUVM) on Verilator by default, `--framework uvm` for the SV-UVM realization on VCS
|Specification |`hw/sys/dtp/doc/index.adoc`: the overview, JTAG-integration, and clock-stop chapters plus the `jtag_intf_unit`, `jtag_ptap`, `jtag_stap`, `cross_trigger_network`, `cross_trigger_port`, and `cross_trigger_matrix` IP chapters
|Registers |JTAG TDR layouts in the `jtag_ptap` architecture chapter; cross-trigger CSRs in the SystemRDL under `hw/ip/cross_trigger/*/regs/`
|Plan |`DTP_VPLAN.adoc` sections 0–6, one procedure per canonical scenario
|Coverage intent |`DTP_FCOV.adoc`: the `cov/sv/` modules (`cover property` points under Verilator, covergroups on the commercial simulators) and the checker-gated 22-cell debug-disable ledger (`env/dtp_fcov.py`); hole dispositions in `cov/config/verilator/coverage_policy.toml`
|Checker |`dtp_scoreboard` with one reference model per feature (`CHK-SB-<FEATURE>`), the `dtp_tap_fsm_checker` and `dtp_scan_window_monitor` subscribers, per-scenario named `CHK-*` evidence finalized once per pass, the passive `ocah_axi_env` per bridge port, and the protocol SVA in `tb_top.sv`; `results.xml` (cocotb) or `UVM TEST PASSED` (SV-UVM) is the run authority
|CI |`.github/workflows/sim.yml` (PR gate) runs `smoke`; `.github/workflows/regress.yml` runs `all` nightly with one seed and weekly with coverage
|===

[[dtp-source-of-truth]]
=== Source of truth

The DV book carries no specification or register reference of its own.
Where the documents overlap, the table names the one that wins.

[cols="2,3,3",options="header",]
|===
|Question |Source of truth |Disposition of the other documents
|What DTP must do |The design documentation under `hw/sys/dtp/doc/` and its IP chapters |`DTP_VPLAN.adoc` cites it and adds no requirement; a plan statement that contradicts a design chapter is a plan defect
|Register and TDR layouts |The `jtag_ptap` architecture chapter (TDRs); the SystemRDL under `hw/ip/cross_trigger/*/regs/` (CSRs) |The DV reference models (`dtp_jtag_ir_model`, `dtp_jtag2axi_model`, `dtp_xtrig_csr_model`) encode these layouts and are checked against them, never the reverse
|Which scenarios exist |`testlists/all.toml` and the per-area lists (`jtag.toml`, `debug_tdr.toml`, `jtag2axi.toml`, `scan.toml`, `xtrig.toml`) |`DTP_VPLAN.adoc` documents every enrolled name; a name in one and not the other is a defect in whichever lacks it
|Which scenarios count |The `all` group, `expected_count`-pinned |Every `[[tests]]` entry belongs to at least one scheduled group, so no declared scenario runs only by name (see <<dtp-credit-rules>>)
|Coverage intent per requirement |`DTP_FCOV.adoc` and the `cov/sv/` modules |This matrix names the covergroup a row leans on; the bin lists stay in `DTP_FCOV.adoc`
|Points that cannot fire |The hole entries in `cov/config/verilator/coverage_policy.toml` |Each entry states the structural reason and the tracker link; this matrix repeats neither
|===

They partition the design documentation; the only design statements outside
them are the SMU-composed IC_RESET external slice and the lifecycle
derivation of `dbg_disable_t`, which are integration-level and out of this
bench.

|`functional` |`smoke` plus the SMC fabric single write and single write-read; bring-up, not a credit set
|`basic_jtag` |F1 credit set: VPLAN section 0
|`debug_tdr` |F2 credit set: VPLAN section 1
|`jtag2axi` |F4 credit set: VPLAN sections 2–4
|`scan` |F3 credit set: VPLAN section 5
|`xtrig` |F5 credit set: VPLAN section 6

`--dut smu` enrols its own DTP-facing scenarios
(`hw/sys/smu/dv/testlists/dtp.toml` and the `dtp_under_smu` group). Those
PASSes are SMU integration evidence and are outside this matrix.

[[dtp-credit-rules]]
=== Credit rules

* Feature credit requires a canonical scenario in `all`, the named evidence
  it declares (`CHK-*` records finalized once per pass, or a required
  `dtp_scoreboard` feature ending as `CHK-SB-<FEATURE>`), and a
  non-vacuous count; the runner accepts a PASS only with positive
  `results.xml` (cocotb) or `UVM TEST PASSED` (SV-UVM) evidence.
* The cocotb and SV-UVM realizations of one name are one scenario. A row is
  credited once; a realization whose evidence differs is recorded in
  <<dtp-gaps>>.
* Compatibility aliases receive no credit and appear in no row.
* Coverage credit is the `cover property` points the `cov/sv/` modules
  record under Verilator; the covergroups add bins on the commercial
  simulators. A point with a hole entry in `coverage_policy.toml` is not
  credited, and its row says so.
* The 22-cell debug-disable contract is credited only through the two
  matrix tests, whose checker-gated ledger refuses to finish with a missing
  cell; the directed gating scenarios corroborate individual cells.
* The formal property modules under `formal/props/` corroborate the rows the
  plan's target table names, the TAP controller module the F1 state-machine
  rows; `run_dv.py --dut dtp --mode formal` runs them as the `*_fpv` items of
  `testlists/formal.toml` (<<dtp-formal-plan>>), and they are not regression
  credit.
* Deriving `dbg_disable_t` from lifecycle policy, the SMU-composed IC_RESET
  external slice, and real SMC and SEP debug TAPs behind the STAPs are
  integration-level: OUT. The downstream TAPs the scan scenarios attach are
  behavioral `ocah_jtag_vip` devices, and the iJTAG chains loop back in
  `tb_top`.

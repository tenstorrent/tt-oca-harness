.SW_RESET_N Register
|===

| 5 | `trng_sw_rst_n` | RW | 0x1 a| Internal TRNG reset request (0=assert, 1=release). Hardware drains and isolates the entropy-source, CSRNG, and EDN AXI ports before asserting the shared TRNG reset.
| 4 | `kmac_sw_rst_n` | RW | 0x1 a| KMAC reset (0=assert, 1=release)

| 2 | `aes_sw_rst_n` | RW | 0x1 a| AES reset (0=assert, 1=release)
| 1 | `otbn_sw_rst_n` | RW | 0x1 a| OTBN reset (0=assert, 1=release)
| 0 | `km_sw_rst_n` | RW | 0x0 a| KM reset (0=assert, 1=release)
|===

SEP eFuse Wrapper Address Map

Places the SEP eFuse controller, its security-token registers and its shadow registers in one 4 KiB page.

Register Arrays:

  • RMA_SIP_TOKEN_I[8]: 8 registers, base 0x400, stride 0x4
  • RMA_CHIPLET_TOKEN_I[8]: 8 registers, base 0x420, stride 0x4
  • SEC_DISABLE_TOKEN_I[8]: 8 registers, base 0x440, stride 0x4

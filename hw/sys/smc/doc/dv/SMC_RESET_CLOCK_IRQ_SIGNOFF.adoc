[[smc-reset-clock-irq-signoff]]
== SMC reset, clock, and interrupt signoff

This chapter defines the reset, clock and interrupt signoff. Feature credit is
the SCOPE matrix in `SMC_SCOPE_TRACEABILITY.adoc`, not every name in
`reset.toml` / `clock.toml` / `irq.toml`. SAMPLE-only observe tests
stay in `functional` (only `smc_cold_reset_test` is also in `smoke`) and are
not requirement rows.

[[smc-rci-credit]]
=== Credited vs observe-only

[cols="2,3,2,1",options="header",]
|===
|Requirement |Test |Group |Credit
|Reset / power-good bring-up |`smc_canonical_smoke_test` |`smoke`, `per_ip` |YES
|Mailbox STATUS / IRQ |`smc_mailbox_irq_test` |`hosted`, `per_ip` |YES
|Function-level reset |`smc_flr_sanity_test` |`hosted`, `per_ip` |YES
|GPIO IRQ active |`smc_gpio_irq_active_test` |`hosted`, `per_ip` |YES
|Multi-reset CSR persist |`smc_multi_reset_csr_persistence_test` |`hosted`, `per_ip` |YES
|Cold reset / clk running / IRQ observe |`smc_cold_reset_test`, `smc_clk_running_test`, `smc_irq_observe_test` |`functional` (`smc_cold_reset_test` also `smoke`) |NO (SAMPLE-only)
|PLL/PVT clock config |none; `smc_pll_*` are dispositioned in `SMC_DEFERRED_DISPOSITION.adoc` |OKAY+0 placeholder wraps |NO
|===

`allow_timeout=True` stays a reviewed exception: helpers in
`smc_csr_seq_utils.py` that set it score the timeout, and each live
call site keeps a rationale comment (README gate). Force / placeholder
paths receive no credit.

Depth groups `reset` / `clock` / `irq` remain debug breadth. They are
not an extra feature-credit set.

On Verilator and Xcelium the `och_prim` `prim_sync2` / `prim_sync3` cells are
replaced by the behavioural stand-ins in `tb/verilator_stubs/`; VCS drops them.
Every leaf of
`reset.toml`, `clock.toml` and `irq.toml` has a row in the *Bench stand-ins*
section of `SMC_DEFERRED_DISPOSITION.adoc` stating which crossing sits on its
proof path, what its verdict reads, and the result of a VCS run on the product
cells. Two runs supply those results. The group control run (`--items reset
clock irq`) covered the 35 leaves of the `reset`, `clock` and `irq` groups: 34
entries of the three files plus `smc_irq_during_powergood_glitch_test`, an
`irq` group member defined in `combined.toml`. The five entries the three
files hold outside those groups -- `smc_jtag_reset_proxy_test`,
`smc_flr_sanity_test`, `smc_multi_reset_csr_persistence_test`,
`smc_zeroer_dma_timeout_test` and `smc_mailbox_irq_test` -- were not in it;
the separate run `20260914_031352__vcs__standin_files_minus_groups`, with the waiver
below in place, grades them 5 of 5 PASS on both the cocotb verdict and the
runner, with no assertion firing after time 0. Two findings of the group
control run belong to this signoff. The first
is closed: the VCS flow graded every leaf FAIL on a time-0 PeakRDL external-ack
assertion class (`assert_bad_ext_wr_ack` / `assert_bad_ext_rd_ack` in
`cpu_ctrl_reg`, `reset_unit_reg` and `system_timer_octs_reg`) that fires once
before reset, while the ack strobes are still X, and that the cocotb verdicts
do not depend on. That class is waived at time 0 only, in `smc_sim_cfg.toml`
`[pass_fail]` in the form SEP uses for `abr_reg`, and the waived re-run
`20260914_035111__vcs__reset` grades 35 of 35 PASS. The second stays open:
`smc_cg_dft_reset_bringup_test` fires the product assertion
`CheckMutualExclusionClk1` in `avsbus_controller.u_refclk_apbclk_mux`
(`prim_ag_clk_mux.sv`) at 4688 ns on seed 1611842931. The waiver does not
cover it -- a replay of the leaf on that seed grades FAIL on that line -- and
no Verilator run can observe it because assertions are compiled out there.
That firing remains an open item of this signoff; the condition that closes
it, and the tracker for it, are the *Closes when* cell of the `prim_sync2/3`
control row in `SMC_DEFERRED_DISPOSITION.adoc`.

The cool-reset OUT names (`smc_cool_reset_from_primary_chiplet_test`,
`smc_cool_reset_from_bmc_test`, `smc_cool_reset_x_cool_reset_test`) have rows
in `SMC_DEFERRED_DISPOSITION.adoc`.

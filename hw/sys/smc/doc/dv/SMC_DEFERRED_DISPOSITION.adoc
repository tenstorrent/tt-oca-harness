[[smc-deferred-disposition]]
== SMC deferred and OUT disposition

This table is the disposition of record for every test name that is
catalogued but not enrolled. Nothing here is enrolled PASS. A name that
still has a body under `hw/sys/smc/dv/cocotb/tests/` restates its blocker on
a leading `# deferred: <tag>` docstring line where a tag applies.

Buckets:

* `adopter-pending OUT` — waits on adopter IP, a product pin, firmware/ROM, or a TB terminator.
* `child-TB-owned` — protocol completeness belongs to a block-level TB (I3C, DTP).
* `waived` — fake BFM, TB-glue, or a commercial alias already covered by a live name.

[[smc-idle-boundaries]]
=== Idle smc_wrapper boundaries (OUT)

These ports have no TB terminator on `--dut smc` / `smc_wrapper`
(`hw/sys/smc/dv/models/README.md`). They are OUT rather than terminated by a
placeholder responder. Block-level I3C and DTP signoff stay on those TBs;
do not count a wrapper terminator as that proof.

[cols="2,2,4",options="header"]
|===
|Boundary |Disposition |Reintroduction trigger
|I3C DAT/DCT |adopter-pending OUT |Real DAT/DCT macros on `smc_wrapper` / `smu_wrapper`. Protocol CCC/IBI remains I3C block DV. Do not credit `smc_i3c_to_fabric_test` as this proof.
|DTP CSR AXI-Lite |adopter-pending OUT |A real DTP CSR slave on `smc_wrapper`, or accept SMU-level DTP tests as the only wrapper-adjacent surface. SMU already wires DTP internally.
|===

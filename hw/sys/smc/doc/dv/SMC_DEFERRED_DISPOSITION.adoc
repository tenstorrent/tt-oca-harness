[[smc-deferred-disposition]]
== SMC deferred and OUT disposition

This is the disposition of record for every test name the package catalogues
without a module under `hw/sys/smc/dv/cocotb/tests/`, and for every enrolled
leaf that no scheduled tier runs (<<smc-scheduled-holdouts>>). Nothing here is
enrolled PASS. Every `@pyuvm.test()` module under `cocotb/tests/` is in at
least one testlist (`smc_base_test` and `smc_dual_base_test` are base classes,
not tests); a catalogued name without a module is one of two kinds:

* A *retired test module* targets this bench and its blocker is
  outside the test: adopter placeholder RTL, a wrapper pin that does not
  exist, an idle boundary, an aperture or window SEP_IN cannot write or
  reach, TB glue, or a VIP the bench lacks. Its code, and
  the `seq_lib/<name>_seq.py` sequence where it had one, is in git history
  and is restored when the blocker named in its row clears.
* A *catalog-only name* never had a module here: a commercial alias whose
  intent a live enrolled name already proves, or a body that needs stimulus,
  RTL or a firmware image this bench does not have.

Buckets:

* `adopter-pending OUT` — waits on adopter IP, a product pin, firmware/ROM, or a TB terminator.
* `child-TB-owned` — protocol completeness belongs to a block-level TB (I3C, DTP).
* `waived` — fake BFM, TB-glue, or a commercial alias already covered by a live name.

[[smc-idle-boundaries]]
=== Idle smc_wrapper boundaries (OUT)

These ports have no TB terminator on `--dut smc` / `smc_wrapper`
(`hw/sys/smc/dv/models/README.md`). They are OUT rather than terminated by a
placeholder responder. Block-level I3C and DTP signoff stay on those TBs;
do not count a wrapper terminator as that proof.

[[smc-bench-tie-offs]]
=== Bench tie-offs

A tie-off is a constant on an `smc_wrapper` input port in `tb_top.sv` (the
single-instance `u_dut` and the `smc_dual_inst` wrapper instance). Every
`--dut smc` / `smc_wrapper` and SMC_DUAL leaf runs under it, on every tool.
Each row states the behaviour the constant keeps out of reach, the enrolled
claims that would change if the pin were driven, and the control that closes
the row.

[cols="2,1,5,3,4,1,1",options="header"]
|===
|Tie-off |Value |Behaviour made unreachable |Tests whose claims would change |Control / closes when |Owner |Review date
|`sep_security_disable_i` (`tb_top.sv`, both `smc_wrapper` instances)
|`1'b0`
|The eFuse sense bypass (`hw/ip/efuse/doc/architecture.adoc`, "Security Disable Bypass Mechanism"). On the product the pin is the SEP `security_disable_o` token-match result (`sep.sv`, wired through `smu.sv`); inside SMC it reaches only `smc_efuse_wrapper` -> `efuse_interface_controller.security_disable_i`. SMC builds the controller with `HAS_LC_STATE=0` (`smc_efuse_wrapper.sv`), so the bypass takes the non-LC branch: the controller drives `shadow_regs_o` from the shadow registers whenever fuse sensing has finished or the pin is high, and drives all zeros otherwise (`efuse_interface_controller.sv:449`); its command mux routes the shadow-register sense FSM to the bank only while sensing is not done and the pin is low (`efuse_interface_controller.sv:759`). With the pin at 1 the FSM's reads are never routed, so `fuse_sense_done` never rises, the shadow registers keep their all-zero reset value and are exposed unsensed, and `fuse_reset_n` -- and with it `rst_warm` (`smc_reset_ctrl.sv:135`) -- never release: the warm domain never leaves reset. With the pin at 0 every run senses the bank, which is what the product does on a chip whose SEC_DIS token did not match.
|None of the enrolled claims: no leaf claims the bypass, and every eFuse leaf proves the sensed path, which is the path a 0 selects. Were the pin driven to 1 for a run, every sequence that calls `wait_fuse_sense_done` (40 sequence files under `cocotb/seq_lib/`; `smc_base_test_seq.py` defines the method and each leaf that needs the warm domain calls it itself) would time out on `tb_fuse_sense_done`, and every warm-domain CSR access would hang with no ready.
|Positive control: a leaf that drives the pin to 1 and requires `fuse_sense_done` to stay 0 while the shadow-register window still answers. It must stay in the cold domain: the eFuse controller sits under `rst_primary`, ahead of `rst_warm`, so its shadow reads through the eFuse CSR window -- over the OTP JTAG AXI-Lite port or the fabric path -- are the only thing that answers under bypass; nothing in the warm domain does. It needs a driven pin (a bench input in `smc_tb_signal_list.svh`, idle 0) in place of the constant. Closes when that leaf is enrolled, or when SEP-integrated signoff (SMU with SEP=1) is accepted as the only place the token-match path is exercised and that acceptance is recorded here.
|SMC DV owner
|2026-12-15

[[smc-bench-standins]]
=== Bench stand-ins

A stand-in is DV-owned RTL that answers in place of a product cell or an
adopter block. Two sit on proof paths of this bench. Each has an inventory
row, a control row, and -- where the stand-in can supply a verdict -- one row
per enrolled leaf stating which crossing is on its proof path, what the
verdict reads, and the result of the control. `hw/sys/smc/dv/README.md`,
`tb/verilator_stubs/README.md` and `models/README.md` describe layout and
enrolment and point here; the dispositions live only here.

Kinds used in the leaf tables:

* *reads the crossing* -- the verdict samples the stand-in's output itself
  (a CSR field or a `tb_top` tap wired to `q_o`). On Verilator that value has
  the stand-in as its author until the pipe has filled.
* *downstream* -- the verdict reads product state that the DUT derives from
  the crossing's output.
* *upstream* -- the verdict reads the near side; the crossing carries the
  value away and nothing behind it is read.
* *none* -- no `prim_sync2` / `prim_sync3` is on the proof path. Listed
  because the audit's reset, clock and IRQ buckets signed off as a whole.

`dual.toml` is therefore in the toolchain bucket, not an "elaboration only"
bucket: all four of its leaves declare `firmware`. `dual` is a tag on those
entries, not a group; `--items` selects the `dual_smoke`, `dual_all` and
`occp_dual` groups.

The `rtl_issue` reproducers are not hold-outs. Every entry carries
`expect_fail` and `expect_fail_match` (`hw/sys/smc/dv/testlists/rtl_issue.toml`),
the weekly tier runs the group on its own `regress.yml` row (artifact
`dv-run-smc-verilator-weekly-rtl_issue`, which the dashboard does not read),
and the runner grades the recorded failure as PASS; an observed PASS grades
`expected_fail_passed`, the signal to move the leaf into its feature testlist.
Members: `smc_deadspace_decode_test` (#585), `smc_remap_cla_test` (#1855: the
CLA unmapped in-window offset `+0x38` completes with OKAY) and
`smc_efuse_map_read_test` (#1989: the shadow map reads 0 after sense, measured
on LOCKS word 0 against the preload image).
`smc_efuse_read_timeout_status_test` is not a member: a read that hits
`EFUSE_READ_REQ_TIMEOUT` completes with `EFUSE_READ_CTRL.READ_STATUS` set, so
the leaf passes, proves `READ_STATUS` 0 -> 1 and runs from `efuse.toml` in
`all` and `hosted`.

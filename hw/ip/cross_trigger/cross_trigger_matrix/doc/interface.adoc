[[ctm-interface]]

=== Signal Interface

Packed control and bus types remain bundles at the `cross_trigger_matrix` module
boundary; their members are not separate scalar ports.

ifdef::backend-html5[]
The parameters and ports of `cross_trigger_matrix` are listed in xref:ocah-docs:ROOT:rtl-modules-reference/ip/cross_trigger_matrix/cross_trigger_matrix.adoc[cross_trigger_matrix].
endif::backend-html5[]
ifndef::backend-html5[]
The parameters and ports of `cross_trigger_matrix` are listed in the HTML edition of this manual, under RTL Modules Reference.
endif::backend-html5[]

`NUM_CT_SRC` and `NUM_CT_DST` come from the generated register package.
Routing masks are programmed through the AXI-Lite CSR port; there are no
separate route-configuration or acknowledge ports.

=== Data Ports

The CTM has the following data ports:

* *CT_Src[N-1:0]* - Cross trigger pulse output signals from the CTM to CLAs,
  CTPs, or other cross trigger sinking devices, where N is the number of these
  cross trigger sinks.
* *CT_Dst[M-1:0]* - Cross trigger pulse input signals to the CTM from CLAs,
  CTPs, or other cross trigger generating devices, where M is the number of
  these cross trigger sources.

=== Dependencies

The CTM depends on the following components:

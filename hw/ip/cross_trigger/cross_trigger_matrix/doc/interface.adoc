=== Signal Interface

These are the ports of `cross_trigger_matrix`. Packed control and bus types remain
bundles at the module boundary; their members are not separate scalar ports.

<<ctm-signal-interface-table>> lists the CTM ports and bundled register interface.

`NUM_CT_SRC` and `NUM_CT_DST` come from the generated register package.
Routing masks are programmed through the AXI-Lite CSR port; there are no
separate route-configuration or acknowledge ports.

=== Data Ports

The CTM has the following data ports:

* *CT_Src[N-1:0]* - Cross trigger pulse output signals from the CTM to CLAs,
  CTPs, or other cross trigger sinking devices, where N is the number of these
  cross trigger sinks.
* *CT_Dst[M-1:0]* - Cross trigger pulse input signals to the CTM from CLAs,
  CTPs, or other cross trigger generating devices, where M is the number of
  these cross trigger sources.

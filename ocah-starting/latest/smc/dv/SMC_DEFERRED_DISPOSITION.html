1. SMC deferred and OUT disposition

This table is the disposition of record for every test name that is catalogued but not enrolled. Nothing here is enrolled PASS. The blocker for each surviving body lives in its own docstring; hw/sys/smc/dv/README.md tabulates the ones under hw/sys/smc/dv/cocotb/tests/.

Buckets:

  • adopter-pending OUT — waits on adopter IP, a product pin, firmware/ROM, or a TB terminator.

  • child-TB-owned — protocol completeness belongs to a block-level TB (I3C, DTP).

  • waived — fake BFM, TB-glue, or a commercial alias already covered by a live name.

1.1. Idle smc_wrapper boundaries (OUT)

These ports have no TB terminator on --dut smc / smc_wrapper (hw/sys/smc/dv/models/README.md). They are OUT rather than terminated by a placeholder responder. Block-level I3C and DTP signoff stay on those TBs; do not count a wrapper terminator as that proof.

Boundary Disposition Reintroduction trigger

I3C DAT/DCT

adopter-pending OUT

Real DAT/DCT macros on smc_wrapper / smu_wrapper. Protocol CCC/IBI remains I3C block DV. Do not credit smc_i3c_to_fabric_test as this proof.

DTP CSR AXI-Lite

adopter-pending OUT

A real DTP CSR slave on smc_wrapper, or accept SMU-level DTP tests as the only wrapper-adjacent surface. SMU already wires DTP internally.

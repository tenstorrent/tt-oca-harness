Project Defines

Compile-time defines that apply across OCAH rather than to a single block.

1. SIMULATION

SIMULATION selects the verification view of OCAH RTL. Set it on every simulation compile and on no other kind of build:

+define+SIMULATION   # VCS, Xcelium
-DSIMULATION         # Verilator, Icarus

OCAH’s own DV flows set it; a compile driven by an adopter’s testbench does not.

Do not let SIMULATION reach synthesis, formal, or a gate-level flow. It enables initial blocks, $readmemh, $random and DPI exports; a tool that skips those rather than rejecting them yields a netlist that does not correspond to the RTL you verified.

Without the define, these testbench hooks are not compiled in and the plusargs are silently ignored:

Plusarg Effect

+skip_fuse_sense

efuse_shadow_regs reports fuse sense complete immediately rather than sensing the bank through the adopter’s SHIM.

+smc_shadow_reg_preload=<file>, +sep_shadow_reg_preload=<file>

Loads the shadow registers from a hex image. Takes effect only alongside +skip_fuse_sense.

+rom_bin64=<file>, +rom_hex=<file>

Preloads OCAH4CORECluster_rom_ext, the SMC CPU ROM simulation model. Applies only to integrations that use the reference memory swaps.

The define also zeroes the RMA and security-disable token and digest bits in efuse_token_processing at time 0, which have no reset and otherwise power up X, and routes OpenTitan assertion failures in the SEP prim_assert shim to $error on non-UVM runs.

Two model families it enables make simulation deliberately non-deterministic: prim_flop_2sync and its Caliptra and Adams Bridge equivalents insert prim_cdc_rand_delay ahead of the synchronizer, and prim_lfsr and prim_double_lfsr randomize their default seed on simulators other than Verilator (+prim_lfsr_use_default_seed=1 pins it). Two runs of one test are therefore not identical unless the simulator seed is fixed; record the seed with any failure you intend to reproduce.

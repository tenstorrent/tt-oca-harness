.High-Level Safety Requirements
[cols="1,4,2", options="header"]
|===
|Requirement ID |Requirement Description |Comments

|OCAH-HSR-GENERAL-001 |OCAH shall retain a functional control core during a systematic or random fault |
|OCAH-HSR-GENERAL-002 |OCAH shall have the capability to hold the chiplet in reset to maintain a safe state after a fault event |
|OCAH-HSR-GENERAL-003 |OCAH shall provide a mechanism for configuring and managing safety relevant configuration |
|OCAH-HSR-GENERAL-004 |When a systematic or random fault event happens, OCAH shall provide a mechanism for signalling critical errors and status outside of its boundaries |Error signals will be routed to SMO which is nominally utside of OCAH
|OCAH-HSR-GENERAL-005 |When a systematic or random fault event happens, OCAH shall provide a reliable mechanism for storing critical errors, error statistics, and error status. |
|OCAH-HSR-SMC-001 |OCAH SMC shall always provide a stable clock and reset for its resources |
|OCAH-HSR-SMC-002 |OCAH SMC shall always allow a stable sensing from external sensors (PVT and telemetry) |
|OCAH-HSR-SMC-003 |When a temperature sensor reports a value exceeding the critical temperature limit, OCAH SMC shall autonomously engage thermal capping to reduce thermal output |Note: bad dynamic power and thermal management (DPTM) in the long term leads to: component’s lifetime shortening, electromigration, dielectric breakdown due to thermal hot spots and sharp thermal gradients, and degraded operating speed due to leakage current exponentially increasing with temperature.
|OCAH-HSR-SMC-004 |When a voltage sensor reports a value exceeding the application power budget, OCAH SMC shall autonomously engage power capping to reduce the total power envelope |
|OCAH-HSR-SMC-005 |OCAH SMC shall prevent operation outside of pre-defined thermal and electrical power boundaries |
|OCAH-HSR-SMC-006 |OCAH SMC shall verify integrity of payload transferred between chiplets |
|OCAH-HSR-SMC-007 |OCAH SMC shall provide memory error detection and correction mechanisms against random faults to ensure data integrity |SECDED
|OCAH-HSR-SMC-008 |OCAH SMC shall provide fabric error detection mechanisms against random faults to ensure data integrity |
|OCAH-HSR-SMC-009 |OCAH SMC shall block unexpected transactions through its fabric |
|OCAH-HSR-SMC-010 |OCAH SMC shall have the capability to block fabric transactions violating the fabric access policy |odd parity
|OCAH-HSR-SMC-011 |OCAH SMC shall detect hangs of OCAH SMC CPU |
|OCAH-HSR-SMC-012 |OCAH SMC shall detect hangs of OCAH SMC fabric and devices connected to the fabric |
|OCAH-HSR-SMC-013 |When PLL and/or VRM actuation is issued from firmware, OCAH SMC shall always guarantee the actuation commands complete as expected |
|OCAH-HSR-SMC-014 |OCAH SMC shall be capable of detecting, reporting, and correcting errors happening during actuation control of PLL and/or VRMs from firmware |
|OCAH-HSR-SMC-015 |OCAH SMC shall ensure all HW sequencers transition within allowed states |
|OCAH-HSR-SMC-016 |OCAH SMC shall ensure transitions to illegal states in HW sequencers are detected and error signals propagated outside of OCAH |
|OCAH-HSR-SMC-017 |OCAH SMC shall detect unexpected interrupt trigger, priority assignment, and routing |
|OCAH-HSR-SEP-001 |OCAH SEP shall provide memory error detection and correction mechanisms against random faults to ensure data integrity |
|OCAH-HSR-SEP-002 |OCAH SEP shall verify the integrity and authenticity of firmware images before allowing execution |
|OCAH-HSR-SEP-003 |OCAH SEP shall prevent execution of firmware versions older than the minimum version recorded in the anti-rollback counter |
|OCAH-HSR-SEP-004 |OCAH SEP shall physically isolate secret key material from the software-accessible data bus to prevent unauthorized key exfiltration |
|OCAH-HSR-SEP-005 |OCAH SEP shall provide an emergency wipe mechanism capable of clearing all secret state (keys, vaults, scrambler state) upon detection of a security-critical fault |
|OCAH-HSR-SEP-006 |OCAH SEP shall ensure that the entropy source and DRBG meet NIST statistical quality requirements and detect degradation via online health monitoring |
|OCAH-HSR-SEP-007 |OCAH SEP shall ensure life cycle state transitions occur only through authenticated token matching with triple-redundant comparison logic |
|OCAH-HSR-SEP-008 |OCAH SEP shall detect inconsistencies in differentially encoded life cycle state and feature control signals |
|OCAH-HSR-SEP-009 |OCAH SEP shall block unexpected or unauthorized inbound and outbound fabric transactions through programmable traffic filtering |
|OCAH-HSR-SEP-010 |OCAH SEP shall ensure all HW sequencers and FSMs transition within allowed states |
|OCAH-HSR-SEP-011 |OCAH SEP shall ensure transitions to illegal states in HW sequencers are detected and error signals propagated outside of OCAH |
|OCAH-HSR-SEP-012 |OCAH SEP shall detect hangs of the OCAH SEP CPU through the Watchdog Timer |
|OCAH-HSR-SEP-013 |OCAH SEP shall detect and report errors in cryptographic operations including bus faults, memory parity errors, and CPU trap events from the Key Manager |
|OCAH-HSR-SEP-014 |OCAH SEP shall prevent unauthorized debug, test, and scan dump access according to the current life cycle state and feature control vectors |
|OCAH-HSR-SEP-015 |OCAH SEP shall ensure that fuse sensing completes successfully before releasing downstream logic and CPU execution |
|OCAH-HSR-SEP-016 |OCAH SEP shall protect OTP shadow registers, token values, and cryptographic keys from exposure through scan chains in all life cycle states |
|OCAH-HSR-SEP-017 |OCAH SEP shall detect unexpected interrupt trigger, priority assignment, and routing |
|OCAH-HSR-SEP-018 |OCAH SEP shall detect hangs of OCAH SEP fabric and devices connected to the fabric |
|===
